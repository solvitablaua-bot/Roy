# Project Overview: Roy's Dino Park

## The idea

Roy, age 6, is the park creator. The website is his dinosaur park builder. He hatches dinosaurs, gives them homes, takes care of them, and opens his park to visitors. Every new thing he does unlocks the next part of the park, so the game keeps growing as he plays.

Roy is also a co-designer. His ideas (new dinosaurs, new places, silly rules) get added to the game over time, so it becomes *his* game.

## Who plays

| | |
|---|---|
| Player | Roy, 6 years old, still learning to read |
| Device | iPad, Safari, touch screen |
| Helper | A parent, for reading and for adding Roy's ideas |

## Look and feel

Inspired by the Jurassic movies, using our own original art:

- **Colors:** deep jungle green, amber yellow, warning orange, dark night sky, stone grey.
- **Places:** a huge wooden park gate, jungle, volcano, river, research lab with glowing eggs.
- **Details:** tall electric fences, yellow and black warning signs, a park map, ranger jeeps.
- **Sound:** roars, stomps, jungle birds, an adventure theme, a big "ta-da" for rewards.
- **Feel:** exciting and a little bit wild, never scary. Dinosaurs are friendly and funny.

## Main parts of the game

1. **Park Gate (home screen):** a giant gate that opens with a roar. Big picture buttons lead to each area.
2. **Egg Lab:** tap an egg to warm it, watch it crack, and a baby dinosaur hatches. Roy picks its color and pattern and names it (or gets a fun random name).
3. **Build the Park:** drag enclosures, trees, water, fences and paths onto the map. Put dinosaurs in their new homes.
4. **Dino Care:** feed (meat or plants), wash, play and put to sleep. Happy dinosaurs dance and give stars.
5. **Ranger Missions:** short tasks such as "Find 3 hidden eggs", "Feed the T-Rex" or "Fix the broken fence".
6. **Dino Book:** a sticker book of every dinosaur Roy has hatched, with a simple fact and sound for each.

## Gamification

- **Stars** for every action, used to unlock new dinosaurs, buildings and decorations.
- **Ranger levels:** Junior Ranger, Ranger, Expert Ranger, Park Boss. Each comes with a new badge and hat.
- **Badges:** First Hatch, Dino Feeder, Fence Fixer, Volcano Explorer and more.
- **Surprises:** a mystery egg each day, dinosaurs that sometimes do something funny.
- **Always winning:** no game over and no losing. Mistakes just get a funny dino reaction and a "try again!"

## Designed for a six-year-old

- Big buttons, at least thumb-sized, with pictures instead of words.
- A friendly guide character speaks the instructions out loud.
- One thing to do at a time, with clear arrows or sparkles showing where to tap.
- Short play sessions that feel complete in 5 to 10 minutes.
- Progress saves automatically on the iPad.

## Safety

- No accounts, ads, chat, in-app purchases or links to other sites.
- Nothing leaves the device. Progress is stored only in the browser.

## Technology

- One website made of plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step.
- Works offline once loaded, on iPad Safari.
- Hosted for free (for example GitHub Pages) so Roy can open it from a home-screen icon.

## Build plan

| Step | What we build | Roy can... |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Park Gate home screen | open the park gate with a roar |
| 2 | Egg Lab | hatch and name his first dinosaur |
| 3 | Dino Book + stars | collect dinosaurs and earn stars |
| 4 | Dino Care | feed and play with his dinosaurs |
| 5 | Build the Park | place enclosures and decorations |
| 6 | Ranger Missions + badges | complete missions and level up |
| 7 | Roy's ideas | add his own inventions to the game |

Each step ends with something Roy can play, so he sees his game grow.
